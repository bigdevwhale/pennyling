# Privacy

Pennyling has no account and no analytics. Your records, accounts, goals, debts, wishes and pets stay on your phone, in the app's own storage. Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on; a backup file you save yourself goes wherever you put it.

Only one thing goes over the network: **the weather for the burrow window.** The coordinates of your town (guessed once from your time zone) are sent to Open-Meteo, or to MET Norway when Open-Meteo can't be reached. Nothing else goes with them.

**The crow** (optional) needs Android's notification access. It reads the notifications only of the apps you mark as banks, takes the amount and the place from them and keeps them on the phone as records waiting for you. From other apps it only notes the app's name when a notification looks like money, so settings can offer it as a bank. Nothing it reads is sent anywhere.

Share cards leave the phone only when you share them yourself.
